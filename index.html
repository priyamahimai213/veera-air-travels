Who We Are

A Local Agency With a Global Reach

Veera Air Travels operates from Thiruthuraipoondi, serving travellers across the Thiruvarur and Nagapattinam belt as well as customers who reach us entirely online.

We are a full-service travel and utility desk. Whether it is a first flight to the Gulf, a student heading overseas, a family holiday, or simply a monthly EB bill, the same team handles it start to finish — no call centres, no ticket numbers, just a person who knows your booking.

  • Personal handling — one point of contact from enquiry to return journey.
  • Transparent pricing — service charges told upfront, never hidden in the fare.
  • Walk-in or online — visit the office or finish everything over WhatsApp.
  • Tamil & English support — explained in the language you are comfortable in.
